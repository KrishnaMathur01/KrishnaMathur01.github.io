Milestone 03 / Engineering Projects

From Theory to Design

Exploring how engineering ideas become CAD models, simulations and mechanical systems.

SolidWorks ANSYS Fluent Machine Dynamics

Designed a fin-and-tube air conditioner heat exchanger using SolidWorks, with copper tubes and aluminum fins. Considered heat-transfer area, airflow resistance and pressure drop.

Used ANSYS Fluent to study compressible flow through parametric nozzle geometries, including Mach contours, pressure distributions, velocity vectors and shock formation.

Modeled a spring-loaded centrifugal governor in SolidWorks. Studied kinematic constraints, equilibrium speed and sleeve travel.

Used SolidWorks to examine frame geometry, rider posture, load paths, structural rigidity and material alternatives.
